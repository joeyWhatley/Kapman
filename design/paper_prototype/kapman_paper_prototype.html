KĀPMAN — Rules

Paper prototype v0.1 · core loop only · 1 player · ~45 min

c. 790. Frisia lies under Frankish rule. You borrowed 100 silver from the Count's reeve at Dorestad to buy a small trading ship. Pay him back in full, in Dorestad, by day 60 — or lose the ship and your freedom.

You need: this packet, one d6, a pencil and eraser, and a coin for your ship. Write everything in pencil.

Setup

  1. Ship coin on Dorestad. Day marker on day 1 (Tracker sheet).
  2. Silver 30. Debt 100. Hull 8, Crew 6, Morale 5. Hold empty (6 lots).
  3. Spring card face up. Shuffle the 6 event cards and draw 1: it applies all Spring.

A turn = one port visit, then one voyage

In port (takes no days)

  1. Arrive. Pay upkeep for the voyage just sailed: 1s per day, plus 1s for each crew above 6. Can't pay? Add the shortfall +2 to your debt.
  2. New season? If the day marker entered a new season since your last port: flip to that season card, add interest (10% of debt, round up), erase all marks on the Market sheet, discard the event and draw a new one.
  3. Market mood. Roll d6 twice. 1st roll: that good +3 here this visit. 2nd roll: that good −3. (Goods are numbered 1–6. Same number twice = no mood.)
  4. Do business, any order: sell, buy, repair Hull (3s/point), hire Crew (5s each, max 8), pay debt (Dorestad only).
  5. Optional: wait a day — +1 day, pay 1s, re-roll market mood.

Voyage

  1. Choose a route from this port (Map). Leaving a Frankish port with swords? Contraband check.
  2. Move the day marker forward by the route's days (+1 if Crew is under 4; +1 in Winter by sea).
  3. Encounter roll (sea routes only): roll d6. Encounter if the roll is ≤ the route's danger: coastal 1, open sea 1–2. Summer and events can raise this. Then roll on the Encounter table.
  4. Arrive at the new port → step 1.

Overland (Ribe–Hedeby): no encounter roll; pay 1s per lot carried for carts.

Encounter table (d6)

1–3Viking raiders — fight (Battle sheet). Winter: Storm instead.
4Storm — Hull −1 and +1 day. Winter: Hull −2.
5Frankish patrol — carrying swords? Contraband check. Otherwise nothing.
6Frisian trader — look at the top event card (put it back). You may swap 1 lot of cargo for 1 lot of any other good.

Prices

Price = base + your marks + season + event + mood. Minimum 1. You buy and sell at the same price, one lot at a time.

  • After each lot you buy, mark +1 on that good at that port.
  • After each lot you sell, mark −1.
  • Marks stay until the next season starts (markets recover).

So 3 lots bought at base 7 cost 7 + 8 + 9 = 24. Big single-good cargoes eat their own profit; mix goods.

Contraband: swords

  • Buy only at Dorestad and Quentovic, max 3 lots per visit. Cannot be sold at Frankish ports.
  • Contraband check every time you leave or enter a Frankish port (Dorestad, Medemblik, Quentovic) with swords aboard, and on a Patrol encounter: roll d6, 1–2 = caught: all swords confiscated, 10s fine (added to debt if you can't pay).
  • Before rolling you may bribe 5s: then you're caught only on a 1.

Ship

  • Hull 0: your ship sinks. Game over.
  • Crew under 4: every voyage takes +1 day.
  • Morale matters only in battle; it resets to 5 at any port.

Seasons & events

Days 1–15 Spring, 16–30 Summer, 31–45 Autumn, 46–60 Winter. Each season card lists its effects. One event is active at a time, for one season. Events are shuffled, not in date order: this is a prototype.

Debt

  • Interest at the start of Summer, Autumn and Winter: +10% of current debt (round up). Paying early saves interest but ties up trading silver.
  • Pay any amount, any time you're in Dorestad.
  • Due: be in Dorestad with the debt cleared by the end of day 60. For missing it, see the Tracker sheet (Reeve's grace).

WIN — Debt cleared in Dorestad by day 60. Score = silver left (compare runs).

BITTER WIN — Cleared during the Reeve's grace (days 61–75).

LOSE — Deadline missed without grace, grace failed, ship sunk (Hull 0), or crew taken in battle (Crew 0).

How to read a price

Price = base + marks + season + event + mood (minimum 1). Same price to buy or sell.

  • Marks: each lot bought here +1, each lot sold here −1. Keep a running total in the dashed box. Erase all at each new season.
  • Mood (on arrival): roll d6 twice. 1st = that good +3, 2nd = −3, this visit only. Goods are numbered 1–6 across the top.
  • Hold: 6 lots total.

The debt

Owed to the Count's reeve at Dorestad. Starts at 100s. Interest +10% (round up) at the start of Summer, Autumn and Winter.

Due: in Dorestad, end of day 60.

Reeve's grace. If you're in Dorestad on day 60 but can't clear the debt, pay at least half of it now. The reeve takes the rest ×1.5 (round up), due in Dorestad by day 75. Play on under Winter rules. Clearing it = bitter win.

Otherwise (not in Dorestad by day 60, can't pay half, or miss day 75): the reeve seizes your ship and you work off the debt in bondage. You lose.

One enemy type: Viking raiders · one d6

Range

Battles start at FAR. Once CLOSE, the range stays close. Some moves need CLOSE.

Round order

  1. You pick one move and resolve it.
  2. If the battle isn't over, the raiders roll d6 on their table.
  3. Check the end conditions.

Morale

  • A side that loses Crew in a round also loses 1 Morale (once per round per side).
  • Morale never goes above 5.

End of battle

Raiders' Morale 0, Crew ≤ 2, or Hull 0They break off. You win. Loot: d6 × 3 silver (none if they sank).
Your Morale 0Your crew surrenders. Raiders take all cargo. You sail on.
Your Crew 0You're taken as slaves. Game over.
Your Hull 0You sink. Game over.
Row Hard succeeds / Tribute paidBattle ends. Voyage continues.

After battle, lost Crew and Hull stay lost until you hire and repair in port.

Raider table (d6, every round)

1Jeer and circle: nothing happens.
2Arrow storm: your Crew −1.
3Close in: range becomes CLOSE. Already close? Treat as 5.
4Ram: range becomes CLOSE. Your Hull −2, their Hull −1.
5Spears: at CLOSE your Crew −2. At FAR they close in instead.
6Board! At CLOSE: boarding fight. At FAR: close in, and a war cry costs you 1 Morale.

Boarding fight

Each side rolls d6 + its current Crew (you roll twice, once per side). The higher total wins. The loser loses Crew equal to the difference (max 3). Tie: both lose 1 Crew.

KĀPMAN — Event cards

Cut along dashed lines · 6 cards · shuffle; draw 1 each season

An event applies until the next season starts, then it's discarded. You'll see 4 of the 6 each run. Dates are real but shuffled: in this prototype, history happens out of order.

KĀPMAN — Playtest log

This is for tuning: write down what felt wrong

Run results

After each run, ask:

  1. Did clearing the debt feel earned, or lucky or trivial? At what day did you know you'd make it (or not)?
  2. Was there a real choice at each port, or one obvious best route?
  3. Did you ever fear a voyage? Which route, and why?
  4. Did you run swords? Was the risk worth it, or never worth it?
  5. Battles: did you ever pick a move besides the best one? Did you flee or pay tribute?
  6. Did any season or event change your plans?
  7. Where was bookkeeping tedious? (That tells the Godot build what to automate or cut.)
  8. Did you pay the debt early to save interest, or hold silver for trading?
