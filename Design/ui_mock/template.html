The rules that make it easy

Left is yours, right is hereYour ship, crew, ledger and chest are always on the left. The port, sail or water you are looking at is always on the right. You never hunt for a panel.
One thing per depthOne drawer, one place, one card. Opening another swaps it. Nothing stacks.
She is never coveredThe middle of the chart is kept clear. When panels open, the chart slides so her mark stays in view.
The clock waits while you thinkA drawer, a place or a card holds the clock. The day stick says so. Only sailing and waiting spend days.
The frame is the table of contentsClick the debt on the tally and the Ledger opens at Debts. Click the fair on the day stick and the chart goes there.
Layers answer one question eachWhere does it pay? What is the sea doing? Whose water is this? What has happened? What do I know? One at a time, and the legend says how old the answer is.
Words before numbersEvery good carries its word: what you saw or were told, by whom, how long ago. The number is there when you saw it yourself.
