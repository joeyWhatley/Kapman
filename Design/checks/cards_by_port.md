# Cards by port, 793 to 805

Made 7 October 2026 from Bake/content/card_census.py (run unchanged; it only reads) and a small scratch script that takes the census's own
lists (cards, open, can_hold) and splits them by port and kind. Nothing here is written by hand: every number and every line of text comes from
the card files in Assets/Kapman/Game/Data/encounters.

What 'open' means is the census's rule: the card's 'when' can hold at that port in some month of 793 to 805, something brings it, and every flag
it asks for can be set by an open card. It is a ceiling (every flag taken as settable, every year as right), not the game's loader.

Kinds: arrival = comes on every call where it holds. drawn = a loose harbour card with a weight (the visit draw). chain step = a card in a
chain_* file, split into those the draw can bring (weight) and those that only follow an earlier step. follow-up = a loose card only named by another card.
'Own' = can open at three of the twelve ports or fewer; 'any-port' = at more than three.
