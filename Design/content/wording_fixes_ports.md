# Wording fixes, ports (first half)

Made 7 October 2026 against `Design/content/lore/period_words.md` part (b).

**Checked:** the cards of `rhine_frisia_793.json`, `england_south_east_793.json`, `neustria_west_793.json`,
`danes_norway_793.json` and `harbour_793.json` whose `when` names one of the twelve first-release ports
(or names no port): 154 cards, 1627 written lines (all slots but `art.*`). `rhineland_flanders_793.json`
has no card at any of the twelve ports (all 40 name other ports), so none of its lines were read.
A script matched whole words from part (b); 185 lines had a hit; each hit was read and judged.

**Not checked:** the cards were not sorted by year, so some rows are cards of later years
(the Tall Dane's bridge, Adelbold's church, Godfred's death) that a 793 to 805 player may never meet.
Only listed words were searched: a modern idea put in other words would be missed.
Each mended line is the old line with only the faulty words swapped; none was read aloud in its card.
Nothing was changed in the game's files.

## Hits read and left alone

- **bank** (59 lines): always the river bank or strand, never a money house. Fine.
- **tide** (27 lines): part (a) says "the flood, the ebb, slack water", but part (b) itself gives "two tides" as
  the way to tell a run, so it was not counted a fault. If the strict rule is wanted, search `tide` in
  rhine_frisia.json (witla.mass_at_the_cross, witla.pilots_shed, witla.fire_on_the_mark, walichrum.plank_coffin,
  walichrum.sods_for_the_ring, traiectum.vecht_crossing, dorestad.breaking_beach, dorestad.first_question),
  england_south_east.json (fordwic, lundenwic, sarre), neustria_west.json (sails_off_the_bar.out_asked),
  danes_norway.json (ripa.dead_guest.dead). "Lammas tide" (sarre.tidburhs_oysters.ship) is the old sense and right.
- **hold** as a verb (hold the lamp, hold the die, hold it against you, the church will not hold them): fine.
- **second**: always "the second one", never the measure of time. Fine.
- **hour** in `fordwic.kings_graves.read` (the ninth hour), `daventre.north_of_the_grave.rung` (wait for the hour)
  and `witla.mass_at_the_cross.tide` (keeps better hours): the hours of prayer. Fine.
- **great** (a great deal, a great beast, the great letter): fine, not the modern cheer.
- **country** (5 lines: doccinga.spring_on_the_mound, doccinga.tafl_at_the_farm.rodulf, dorestad.ships_overland.rope,
  traiectum.odulfs_promise.now, traiectum.priest_for_the_ems.known): means the land, not a state. Left; "land" would be safer.
- **freedom / rights** (daventre.bishops_swallow.freedom, medemelacha.ansfrieds_share, witla.five_cows.eleven):
  a granted freedom from toll, and a bondwoman's freeing: things, not the idea. Fine.
- **England** in `ripa.writ_at_the_door.pennies` (a Northman says *England*: part (c) allows it) and in
  `fordwic.brumans_customs.dofras` (the Dover men quit of toll: an 11th-century matter). Fine.
- **port** (port-reeve, his port is open, prefect of the port): the trading place, not the ship's side. Fine.
- **money** in `dorestad.kings_mint.riddle` and `.do_answer` ("what money was before it was money"): the riddle is
  about the idea itself, so "silver" would spoil it. Left.
- **guard / guards / judge** as verbs; **staff** (a walking staff); **wheel** (of cheese); **fork** (of the river);
  **cool** (the weaving house): fine.
- No hit at all in these 1627 lines for: hello, goodbye, please, sorry, okay, captain, deck, cabin, mile(s), minute,
  Viking, Denmark, Norway, France, prison, religion, Bible, dinner, tax, profit, customer, map, compass.
