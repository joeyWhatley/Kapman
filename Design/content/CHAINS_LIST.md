# The chains being written tonight

- house_debt (house): The 480 pennies owed to the count of Dorestad: from the first Michaelmas to the count's friendship, his enmity, or freedom bought another way. The start of the game.
- house_marriage (house): A marriage for the house: a match sought among Frisians, Franks, English or Danes, the bride price and morning gift, kin who must agree, and what the alliance opens.
- house_rival (house): A rival Frisian house that grows as the player does: undercutting, a lawsuit at the thing, a wrecked boat, and an end in ruin, in partnership or in a feud that outlives both men.
- house_heir (house): An heir: fostered abroad to learn a tongue, a first voyage, a quarrel, and the day the father must hand over the steering oar.
- house_new_ship (house): A bigger ship built over a winter: oak from up the Rhine, a wright who will not be hurried, iron for the rivets, wool for a sail from the Frisian weavers, and the launching.
- black_stones (trade): The quarrel of Charlemagne and Offa that shut the ports about 790, and the letter of 796 about black stones (querns) and the length of cloaks: a Frisian caught between two kings.
- silver_road (trade): The road east to Ladoga and the silver of the Caliphate: the first dirhams in the Baltic about 800, the Rus, furs and slaves, and what the silver costs.
- sword_smuggle (trade): Frankish swords going north against the king's bans of 779 and 805: a smith on the Rhine, a buyer at Sliesthorp, the toll man, and the day a sold blade comes back.
- wine_fleet (trade): The wine fleet to the fair of Saint-Denis in October: Frisians and English on the Seine, the abbey's tolls, a partner's casks, and a winter's profit or a spoiled cargo.
- walrus_ivory (trade): Walrus ivory and hide rope from the far north, as Ohthere told King Alfred about 890: a Norse lord's tribute from the Finnas, and a market for it in the south.
- amber_road (trade): Amber from the Ests at Truso, as Wulfstan saw them about 890: their ale, their funeral races, their frozen dead, and a trade that has to be learned.
- riddle_trail (mystery): A riddle-setter whose riddles lead from port to port (use real ids from riddles_793.json with give_riddle): who he is, why he sets them, and what lies at the end.
- lindisfarne_book (mystery): A gospel book carried off from Lindisfarne on 8 June 793, seen for sale in the north: found, bought, stolen or left, and what the bishop will do for the man who brings it home.
- dirham_hoard (mystery): A dying Gotlander's account of silver buried on his farm, and the kin who also know. Hoards were buried and not all were dug up again.
- relic_theft (mystery): An abbey wants a saint's bones and does not ask how they come: the sacred thefts of the ninth century (Einhard's own account of 827), told from the boat that carries them.
- barrow (mystery): A broken burial mound on a headland, and what a crew does when gold shows in the earth. The Christian and the heathen of the crew do not agree.
- lost_brother (mystery): A kinsman taken in a raid and sold: the slave road by Sliesthorp and Birka east, or south to Verdun and Spain, followed port by port.
- mission_north (faith): Carrying the mission north: Anskar to Birka in 829 (pirates took his books on the way) and again about 852; the prefect Hergeir; the lot cast at the thing over whether to allow the new god.
- old_gods (faith): A vow made to the sea in a bad hour, and the price of keeping it and of breaking it: Nehalennia's altars in the dunes, a blot in the north, a priest who will hear of it.
- pilgrim_road (faith): Pilgrims for Rome by way of Quentovic, the English road: a passenger with a secret, tolls and hosts, and what a pilgrim brings back.
- the_bell (faith): A bell for a new church: the metal, the founder, the carrying of it by water, and the first time it is rung (Anskar's churches had bells that the heathen feared).
- kentish_revolt_796 (war): Kent rises against Mercia under Eadberht Praen, 796 to 798, and the Wantsum ports are caught in it: whom to sell to, and what comes after when Coenwulf wins.
- saxon_exiles_804 (war): The last deportations of the Saxons beyond the Elbe in 804: a family that wants passage, the count's men who watch the rivers, and a people that remembers who helped.
- reric_808 (war): King Godfred destroys Reric in 808 and carries its merchants to Sliesthorp: getting out with the goods, or going with them to the new town.
- coast_watch_810 (war): Godfred's two hundred ships in Frisia in 810 and the hundred pounds of silver paid; Charlemagne's fleet and coast watch of 800 to 811, which wants boats and men.
- dorestad_834 (war): The first sack of Dorestad in 834 and the three after it: a warning, a wall, a ransom, a flight up the river, or a storehouse in ashes. Real history that the player may change.
- great_army_865 (war): The Great Army in England from 865: trading with the army's camp, ransoming captives, Alfred and Guthrum's peace, and the merchants allowed to cross between them.
- rorik_850 (later): Rorik the Dane holds Dorestad from the emperor from 850: a heathen lord over a Christian port, and a merchant who must be his man or not.
- rollo_911 (later): The Seine in the hands of Northmen, and Rollo made lord at Rouen in 911: a ruined market that becomes the best on the Channel.
- york_954 (later): York between the kings of Dublin, Eric Bloodaxe and the English, to 954: the richest market in the north and the hardest to keep a friend in.
- maldon_991 (later): The tribute years from 991: English silver flowing to the fleets, and the merchants who follow it; St Brice's day 1002.
- cnut_1016 (later): Cnut's peace after 1016, one king from the Thames to the Sound, and his letter of 1027 on the tolls taken from merchants and pilgrims.
- tiel_1020 (later): The merchants of Tiel about 1020, who "swear and drink together" and judge their own cases, as the monk Alpert complained: a seat among them.
- year_1066 (later): Three fleets in one year: Harald Hardrada, William, and Harold's ships that could not be kept at sea. What the house does in 1066 is the end of the game.
- wreckers (sea): A light on a shore where no light should be, wreck law and who owns what the sea gives up: the wreckers, the lord of the strand, and the crew of a lost ship.
- storm_vow (sea): A vow made in a storm to a saint (or to Thor) and the long way to keeping it: a candle the height of a man, a pilgrimage, a gift that must be the best thing in the hold.
- pilots_secret (sea): An old pilot who knows the bars from the Maas to the Vlie and will teach one man before he dies: his price is not silver.
- saga_rhine_frisia (saga): A saga of one region, played among its ports (dorestad, traiectum, witla, walichrum, noviomagus, daventre, medemelacha, wijnaldum, doccinga, emuthon): choose the best true story the region has that no other chain tells (read its dossiers in Design/content/dossiers), and make its people known in more than one port.
