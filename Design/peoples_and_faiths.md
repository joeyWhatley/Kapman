# Peoples and faiths: what could be added

The user, 7 Oct 2026: "What're all possible cultures and religions and sub religions to be added?"

Written from memory, not from a fresh reading of sources: before any of it goes into the game each line needs its
source, as the rest of the data has. "From" is the year a people or a faith first matters to a merchant of these seas.
Where the record of a belief is thin, it says so.

**In the game today** (Assets/Kapman/Game/Data/story/self_793.json):
peoples Frisian, Frank, English, Dane (first release), Norseman, Svea or Gotlander, Wend, Irish (later);
faiths Christian of Rome, Christian of the Irish houses, the old gods, prime-signed, the gods of the Wends, a Jew
(a merchant of the palace), a Muslim of al-Andalus, a Greek Christian.

## What I would add, and when

- **For the first release (793 to 805, twelve ports):** the old Saxons and their gods (the war is next door, and
  exiles and captives pass through Dorestat); the Frisians' own old faith, fading; the Britons as traders in Kent's
  ports; the half-way Christian. All four are already implied by cards that exist.
- **With the north and the Baltic:** the Balts, the Finnic peoples and the Sami; the gods of the Wends by temple; the
  old gods by the god a man holds to.
- **With the river road:** the Rus, Khazars, Volga Bulgars; Tengri; the Khazar court; Sunni by school; the Church of
  the East.
- **With Spain and the Middle Sea:** the peoples of al-Andalus, the Spanish rite and the Adoptionists, the Ibadi and
  Sufri merchants, the Greeks' quarrel over images, the eastern churches.
- **With the later years:** Normans, Anglo-Danes, Icelanders, Cluny, the Slavonic rite, Bogomils, the Fatimids, the
  break of 1054.
- **With the far west:** the Dorset and Thule peoples and the peoples of Vinland, with advice.

A kind within a faith should only be added where it changes what a merchant can do: whom he may eat with, swear with,
marry, bury beside; which days are shut; what he may carry; where he is safe. Otherwise it is a name in the wiki, not
a choice at the start.
