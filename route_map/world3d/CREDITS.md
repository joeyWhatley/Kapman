# 3D world data credits

**Elevation, Frisian coast slice** (`route_map/data/slice_height.exr`, baked by `route_map/tools/bake_slice.gd`)
from [AWS Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/) (Mapzen / Tilezen joerd), zoom 11.
Its sources include:

- SRTM, NASA / USGS (public domain)
- ETOPO1, NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (public domain)
- EU-DEM, produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union
- GMTED2010, USGS (public domain)
- and national datasets listed at https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

The in-game credits must carry: *"Elevation: Mapzen Terrain Tiles; contains SRTM (NASA), ETOPO1 (NOAA),
EU-DEM (produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union) and other sources."*

**Whole-map elevation** (`route_map/data/terrain_height.exr`): NOAA ETOPO1 (public domain).

**Coastline, lakes, rivers**: Natural Earth 1:10m (public domain).
